- **Watermark PDF** stamps text or a picture across the pages of a document, on your device. Choose a word like DRAFT or CONFIDENTIAL, or a logo from your device, set its position, size, turn and strength, and name the pages it goes on. The mark is drawn onto the pages rather than the pages being rebuilt, so the text under it stays selectable, the fonts stay embedded, and the file barely grows.
- The mark is shown over the page before anything is written, drawn from the same placement the document is written with — so the preview is where it goes, not an impression of it. Turn to a page outside the selection and the mark disappears with a line saying why.
- A page carrying its own rotation is corrected for. A sideways scan — or a page turned in Organize PDF — is displayed with its sides swapped, and a mark placed in the corner you are looking at lands in that corner rather than off the edge of the page.
- Watermark PDF joins Gizlet Flows as a `pdf-file` → `pdf-file` block. A recipe link carries one of five words, a position and a strength, because every value that format holds is a whole number or a name from a closed list and never anything you typed. The workspace itself takes any text.
- A visible watermark marks a document; it does not protect one. Nothing on the page claims otherwise, and the questions on it say so plainly.
- The shared PDF viewer gained a `page-overlay` slot — a hole rather than a feature, for the Gizlets that put something *onto* a page and have to show where it will land.
