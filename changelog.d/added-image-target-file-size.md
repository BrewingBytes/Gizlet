- Compress Image can now work to a file size instead of a quality. Choose 100 KB, 200 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB, or type your own whole number of KB, and your browser tries descending qualities until the picture comes in under it. In this control KB means 1,000 bytes and MB means 1,000,000, which is what an upload form printing a limit means.
- The result says how many bytes it actually landed on and whether the limit was met. A picture that will not come down that far is offered as a clearly labelled best attempt beside a link to Resize Image, rather than being quietly switched to another format or made smaller.
- A batch applies the limit to each picture on its own and reports met, unmet, or a failure for every row. PNG has no quality to trade away, so it stays on the slider and the settings now say why, and a run that is taking too long can be stopped without losing the settings it was given.
