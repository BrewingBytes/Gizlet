- **Organize PDF** rebuilds a document by its pages, on your device. Drop a PDF and every page is drawn as a card you can drag somewhere else, turn a quarter at a time, copy, or drop, with the new order and orientation shown as you build it. Saving writes a fresh PDF holding the pages exactly as they sit on screen; the file on your device is never touched. The pages are copied rather than redrawn, so the text stays selectable, the fonts stay embedded, and nothing is re-encoded.
- Every drag has a button beside it. Arrow controls move a page one place at a time, so a whole document can be reordered from the keyboard, and each card says both where its page will be and which page of the original it came from — a document you have rearranged still reports what it was made of.
- A turn is added to the way a page already sat rather than replacing it, which is what makes a sideways scan upright after one press instead of sending it back where it started. A copy lands directly after the page it copies and is independent from then on, so it can be moved and turned without touching the original.
- Tick pages to act on several at once — turn them, copy them, drop them — or extract them into a second PDF holding only those pages, in the order and orientation you left them in. The last page cannot be dropped, because a PDF with no pages is not a PDF, and a document past the page count the PDF Viewer opens is refused with both numbers rather than attempted.
- Organize PDF joins Gizlet Flows as a `pdf-file` → `pdf-file` block, and a recipe link carries the one page job that needs no field: the turn every page gets. Where a page goes is a drag on a document nobody else is looking at, so it is deliberately not something a link can carry.
